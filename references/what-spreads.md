# What makes these games spread

Drawn from about 2,400 public posts read on 9 and 10 November 2026, and live counts from that date. Everything here is observed from a selected sample. It describes what happened, without a controlled test of why. Build times and bills are author reports.

## What the numbers show

- **Video is nearly universal.** Every top-level game post above 3,000 views in the sample had gameplay video, 20 to 147 seconds long. Those with the most views per follower ran 57 and 100 seconds.
- **Receipts travel.** Hours, tokens and dollars get quoted. "$550, 8 hours, 2.7M output tokens" was repeated by larger accounts; "40% of Max 5x usage" and "$12 in API costs" did the same job in June.
- **The prompt is a second post.** One launch's prompt reply did 15,944 views on its own, and a larger account quoting the same text did 16,717 more.
- **The link works in the main post or first reply.** Both happened in the top posts.
- **Concept beats genre.** A boat through eight dreamlike worlds, a reversed haunted-mansion mystery, the player as the monster, a work ticket turned into horror. A block-world clone did 3.27 million views in June and about 1,000 five months later.
- **Follower count predicts little.** 416k followers got 22,922 views; 24k got 184,732; 199 got 188,500; 96 got 29,526.
- **A visual comparison works at any account size:** same prompt, two models, both bills.

## Launch-day timing

Counted from the moment a major model is announced:

- First hour: mostly people with early access.
- 2 to 9 hours: a vivid concept can still break out.
- From about 4 hours: the generic version stops working. Four accounts with 11,000 to 34,000 followers posted it and got 770 to 1,650 views each.

Publish within the window, with something only you would make.

## Patterns worth copying

| Pattern | Example | What makes it work |
|---|---|---|
| Same game, two models, two bills | [Space Train](https://x.com/EnvolDev/status/2110116583016419760): $3.40 and 4m20s vs $23.46 and 113m | The viewer weighs a visible result against a real tradeoff |
| A place or object that is personal | [Toronto pigeon world](https://x.com/yegor/status/2119569510810177766), [a remembered pinball table](https://x.com/scheemunai/status/2104475552660824147) | A reason to care before the model is named |
| A playable joke, a reversed role | [reverse mystery](https://x.com/emollick/status/2119876449494540554), [ticket-queue horror](https://x.com/emollick/status/2115981377652822479) | One sentence, instant play |
| A gallery with the recipe beside each result | [a film and prompt gallery](https://x.com/oozn/status/2112877565979308529) | Browse results and copy the input without hunting replies |
| Show the checks and give them away | [audit prompt with release traps](https://x.com/LimestoneHQ/status/2108174605722526126) | Worth saving, not only watching |
| Local humour | [Bucharest satire game](https://x.com/le_alecs/status/2083570326378569785), 363,624 views from 128 followers | The local audience shares it |

## What the evidence does not show

- That more money or time gives a better game. Spend varied by a factor of more than 100 without ranking quality.
- That reusable pieces explain anyone else's eight-hour build, or how much time they save. Only a matched run, same prompt and model with and without the pieces, can show that.
- Which bookmark or view counts come from the game and which from the account.
