# Playable gallery

23 games made with coding agents, kept after the reference audit of 10 Nov 2026 (`references/AUDIT.md`). Each was graded against the best one-shot browser game we know of: **A** looks AAA in motion, **B** has a part worth taking. 67 entries were removed: dead links, clones of named games, pages that are not games, and trivial games. Links only: nothing here is copied, and every game belongs to the person credited.

Screenshots are in `references/audit/gallery/`. Most were taken from the post's video thumbnail, because Claude artifact links stop at a bot check and many heavy pages draw nothing on software graphics; the `graded_from` field in [`games.json`](games.json) says which.
