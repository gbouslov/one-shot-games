# Caveman first-person game

An original concept, ready to run. No timed run of this prompt exists yet.

```text
make me a caveman FPS
```

A fuller version for use outside this repo:

```text
Build a small first-person game set at the end of an ice age. You are the last hunter of your clan, guarding the one fire left in a snowbound valley through the longest night. A throwing spear that sticks in what it hits, a heavy club and burning brands you can drop to hold ground. Invented beasts, not real prehistoric species and not creatures from any existing game, come out of the dark toward the light. If the fire dies you lose. Survive to dawn and win. Low firelight against blue snow, drifting ash, breath in the cold. Small in scope, one valley, a five-minute round, and finished.
```
