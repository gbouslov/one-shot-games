# Original concepts

One per line, written to be run as they are, and none tested yet. Each is shaped after something that spread (`references/what-spreads.md`) without borrowing from any existing game. For a launch day, pick three in advance.

## Short and demanding

```text
Build me a first-person game about the last lighthouse keeper on a sea that has started to rise. Make it small in scope but make every frame look expensive.
```

```text
A tiny first-person horror game in a flooded office at night where the only light is your phone. Small in scope, impossible to look away from.
```

```text
Make a first-person game where you are a bee defending a hive the size of a cathedral. One room, spectacular light.
```

```text
Build the most beautiful five-minute arena game you can, set on the back of a walking mountain.
```

## The player is the thing you'd expect to fight

```text
You are the haunted house. Visitors walk in. Slam doors, move rooms and flicker lights to scare them out before they reach the attic. Score by how fast they run.
```

```text
You are the final boss of a game, in first person. Tiny heroes keep arriving. Defend your treasure room through five waves of them.
```

```text
You are the storm. Push a fleet of small boats onto the rocks before they reach harbour.
```

## Spectacle you can break

```text
A floating island held up by balloons. Pop them to bring buildings down. Debris changes where you can stand. Try to reach the top before it all falls.
```

```text
A tiny physics toy: a tower of glass and iron in a desert. One cannon. Every shot should look like a film.
```

## Personal

```text
Here is a photo of my desk. Turn it into a tiny racing track with objects from the photo as obstacles and a car I can drive.
```

```text
Here is my drawing. Make it the boss of a one-room fight, with animations that keep the character of the sketch.
```
